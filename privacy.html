Element Match Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Element Match is a puzzle game. It has no accounts of its own and no ads. Its only in-app purchase, where it is offered, is the optional paid reward track of an Adventure chapter. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. The same text is shown in the game under About and on the game's App Store page.

What stays on your device

Your best scores, your Adventure progress (passed levels and events, coins and boosters, which paid reward tracks you own) and your sound, music, vibration, hint and analytics settings are saved on your device only. Deleting the game deletes them.

Anonymous gameplay analytics

To learn which parts of the game are fun and which are confusing, the game sends anonymous usage events to our analytics provider, PostHog. These include:

  • game events, such as starting and finishing a game, the score, moves, boosters and specials used, and reading the rules;
  • Adventure events, such as which level or event you played, how many times and for how long, whether you won, progress toward its goals, the boosters you used, the in-game coins you earned and spent (level prizes, the welcome gift, extra moves and boosters), which booster offers in the coin shop you saw and bought, and which in-level tutorial steps you saw or skipped;
  • purchase events, described under In-app purchases below;
  • app events, such as opening and closing the app, session length and the screen you were on;
  • basic technical details: device model, iOS version, app version, language, time zone and how smoothly the game ran (frame rate);
  • performance diagnostics, described in the next section;
  • a random identifier created when the game is first opened, used to count returning players. It is not linked to your name, email, Apple Account or advertising identifier.

When the game sends data, PostHog sees your device's IP address as part of the internet connection. Our project is set to discard it: the address is not stored and is not used to work out your location.

You can turn analytics off at any time: open About (the i button in the main menu) and switch off Share anonymous analytics. After that, the game sends nothing.

Performance and crash diagnostics

Some phones get hot or stutter while playing, and we want to find out why. For that the game keeps a small diagnostics log on your device. When iOS reports that the device is warming up, and once a minute while it stays warm, the game writes down the temperature state, which screen was open, the frame rate it asked for and got, how many objects were on the board, how much processor time and memory the game itself used, the battery level and whether Low Power Mode was on. The game also records when it froze for more than a second, and it receives iOS's own reports about crashes and freezes of the game (Apple's MetricKit: how long the freeze lasted, where in the game's code it happened, and the day's totals of processor, graphics and screen time). These reports are about the game's code only; they contain no messages, photos, contacts or anything else from your device.

While Share anonymous analytics is on, these records are sent to PostHog with the other analytics events, anonymously and without an identifier of yours. With the switch off, nothing is sent; the log stays on your device and is deleted with the game. In About you can also press Send diagnostics to share the log yourself, for example by email, and see everything it contains.

In-app purchases

Each Adventure chapter can have an optional paid reward track: one purchase per chapter, made through Apple's App Store, which adds extra rewards (coins and boosters) to the steps of that chapter. Payment is handled entirely by Apple: we never see your name, Apple Account, payment method or billing details. The game only receives Apple's signed confirmation that this Apple Account owns the track, checks Apple's signature on your device, and remembers on your device which tracks you own. Restore Purchases asks the App Store for the purchases of your Apple Account again. A refunded purchase no longer adds new rewards; rewards it already gave stay.

While Share anonymous analytics is on, the game sends anonymous purchase events with the other analytics: which chapter's track you opened the purchase for, whether it was completed, cancelled, pending or failed, the price and currency the App Store showed, refunds and restores. These events carry no transaction or order number and nothing about your payment or Apple Account.

Game Center leaderboards

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, the game can post your scores to Game Center leaderboards (Classic and Timed by day, week, month and all time; your highest Adventure level), where other players see them next to your Game Center nickname. Signing in is up to you: the game never asks in the middle of play, only when you open the leaderboards.

Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Your scores go to Apple, not to us: the game does not read, store or send your Game Center name or player ID anywhere, including to our analytics. Before a score is posted, the game replays the moves of that game on your device to check the score; this check stays on your device.

You can sign out of Game Center or limit who sees your profile in the Settings app under Game Center.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • We don't use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Where the data is processed

PostHog, Inc. stores analytics data on our behalf on servers in the United States. We keep it only as long as it helps us improve the game.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The analytics described above are anonymous and can be turned off.

Questions and changes

If you have a question or a request about this policy, contact us through the support link on the game's App Store page. If the policy changes, the new version will appear in the game and on the App Store page with a new effective date.
