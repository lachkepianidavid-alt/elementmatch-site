Element Match — Support

Element Match is a match-3 puzzle game for iPhone by Saluka Games. No account is needed and there are no ads.

Contact us

Questions, bug reports or ideas: write to lachkepiani.david@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version; if the game froze or crashed, open Settings → About → Send diagnostics in the game and attach the log — it contains only technical details about the game itself.

Email support

Common questions

Where is my progress saved? On your device only. Deleting the game deletes your Adventure progress, coins and boosters; there are no accounts or cloud saves yet.

How do I turn analytics off? Settings → About → switch off Share anonymous analytics. The game then sends nothing. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Sound, music or vibration? The gear button in the main menu opens Settings, where each can be switched separately. Distinct shapes (marks on gems for colour-blind players) is in Settings → Accessibility.

A level seems impossible. Granite (from level 202) only breaks with a special gem or a booster; a plain match next to it does nothing. Colour locks (from level 102) open with a match of their own colour next to a crate. Every level has been checked by a bot, but tell us the level number if you are stuck — we re-balance levels regularly.

In-app purchases. Coin packs and the paid reward track of an Adventure chapter are optional and bought through the App Store. To get a reward track back on a new device, open the track screen and tap Restore Purchases. For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem page.

Leaderboards. Classic and Timed have Game Center leaderboards for today, this week, this month and all time. Tap the trophy in the main menu or next to your best score on the result card. To appear on them, sign in to Game Center in the iOS Settings app (Settings → Game Center); other players see your Game Center nickname next to your score, and the game itself never reads either. Signing in is optional, and the game plays the same without it.
